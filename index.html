Privacy Policy for Esin

Last updated: 31-5-2026

Esin is a language-learning app that helps you practice writing in English. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and your rights over it.

The app is built and operated by an individual developer based in the United Kingdom. In this policy, "we" and "I" mean that developer. You can contact me at tyanik.tycae@gmail.com .

TL;DR — the short version
  • Esin has no accounts, no login, and no advertising.
  • When you tap "Get AI feedback," the text you wrote is sent over the internet to be checked by an AI. Once the feedback comes back, your text is not stored by us.
  • Your settings (chosen language, interests, level, streak) are saved only on your own phone. They never leave your device.
  • We don't sell or share your data with marketers.

1. What data Esin handles

1.1 Data stored only on your phone

The following is saved by the app in your phone's local storage and never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your chosen feedback language
  • Your chosen interface language for word meanings
  • Your selected interests
  • Your difficulty level
  • Your daily streak count and the date of your last submission
  • Daily usage counters
  • Theme preference (light/dark/system)
  • A randomly generated app identifier used only to apply daily usage limits

If you uninstall the app, all of this is deleted with it.

1.2 Data sent over the internet when you request AI feedback

When you tap "Get AI feedback," the app sends the following to our server (a Cloudflare Worker we operate):

  • The 3 vocabulary words for that session (from our built-in word list — not personal data)
  • The text you wrote (typically 5 - 80 words)
  • Your chosen feedback language
  • Your chosen interests, if any
  • A randomly generated app identifier (not linked to your real identity) used to enforce fair usage limits

Our server forwards this to Anthropic (the company that makes the AI model, Claude) to generate the feedback. The feedback is returned to your phone and shown to you. We do not save your text, your feedback, or this data on our server after the request is complete.

1.3 Data automatically logged by our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare)

Cloudflare, our infrastructure provider, automatically logs basic request metadata for security and abuse prevention, including your IP address, approximate location derived from IP, the time of the request, and standard request headers. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

1.4 Data processed by Anthropic (the AI provider)

The text you write is sent to Anthropic's API to generate feedback. We recommend you review Anthropic's privacy policy directly for full details, as their practices may change.

2. What Esin does NOT collect

To be explicit, the app does not collect or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account information (the app has no accounts)
  • Your precise location (GPS)
  • Your device advertising ID
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, or files
  • Analytics events or behavior tracking
  • Any data for advertising purposes

The app contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

3. Why we handle the data we do

  • Your written text — to generate the AI feedback that is the core purpose of the app.
  • Language and interests — to make the AI feedback relevant and in the right language.
  • App identifier and IP/metadata — to protect the service from abuse (rate-limiting).

We do not use any of this data for marketing or profiling.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with:

  • Cloudflare — our hosting and infrastructure provider.
  • Anthropic — the AI provider whose model generates the feedback.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • On our server: not retained — only briefly held in memory while the request is processed.
  • By Cloudflare and Anthropic: subject to their retention policies (linked above).

6. Security

All data sent between your phone and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We don't store user text on our servers, so there is no database of your writing to be breached. Local storage on your phone is protected by Android's app sandboxing.

7. Your rights

If you are in the UK, EEA, or another jurisdiction with similar laws, you have the right to ask what data is held about you, request correction or deletion, object to or restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Because Esin has no accounts and does not store your writing on our server, in most cases there is no personal data on our side to access or delete. To remove data stored on your phone, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

8. Children

Esin is a language-learning app suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and the app has no features that require personal data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change.
